# Church Website - Planning Document

## Overview

This document outlines the structure, pages, and content for a Christian church website with both public-facing pages and a secure member portal.

---

## PART 1: PUBLIC PAGES

### 1. Home Page

**Purpose:** First impression, welcoming visitors, and directing them to key areas.

**Content to include:**
- Hero section with church name, tagline, and main call-to-action (e.g., "Plan Your Visit")
- Service times and location (prominent)
- Welcome message or short intro video from the pastor
- Upcoming events highlight (2-3 featured events)
- Latest sermon preview with link
- Quick links: New Here, Watch Online, Give, Contact
- Social media links
- Newsletter signup form

---

### 2. About Us

**Subpages:**

#### 2.1 Our Story / History
- Church founding story
- Timeline of milestones
- Mission statement
- Vision statement

#### 2.2 What We Believe (Statement of Faith)

- Biblical foundations
- Denominational affiliation (if any)

## PART 5: DESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS

### Visual Elements
- Clean, modern design
- Church branding (logo, colors)
- High-quality photography
- Consistent typography
- White space for readability
- Mobile-responsive design

### User Experience
- Clear navigation (max 7 main items)
- Prominent search function
- Fast loading times
- Easy-to-find contact info
- Clear calls-to-action
- Minimal clicks to key content

### Trust Signals
- SSL certificate
- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- Secure giving badges
- Staff photos (real people)
- Testimonials

---

## PART 6: TECHNICAL NOTES

### Recommended Tech Stack Options
- **Frontend:** React, Next.js, or Vue.js
- **Backend:** Node.js, or headless CMS
- **Database:** PostgreSQL or MongoDB
- **Authentication:** Auth0, Firebase Auth, or custom JWT
- **Hosting:** Vercel, Netlify, or AWS
- **CMS:** Sanity, Strapi, or Contentful

### Security Considerations
- HTTPS everywhere
- Secure authentication
- Role-based access control
- Data encryption
- Regular backups
- GDPR/privacy compliance
